## About Me

I build systems that turn data into decisions and ideas into reality.

I'm a Data Engineer and Software Engineer passionate about designing scalable architectures, building data platforms, and engineering high-performance systems. My work lies at the intersection of software engineering, data, business strategy, and artificial intelligence — transforming complex problems into practical, reliable, and meaningful solutions.

But engineering, to me, is more than writing code.

I believe great engineering begins with clear thinking. That simplicity is a form of sophistication, architecture is an investment in the future, and technology should serve a purpose beyond its own existence.

Inspired by Stoic philosophy, particularly the writings of Seneca, I value discipline over impulse, substance over appearance, and continuous improvement over the pursuit of perfection.

I believe in **Clean Architecture** as a foundation for systems that endure, data as a strategic asset rather than just stored information, and ownership as a commitment to seeing things through.

My work spans three interconnected disciplines:

- **Data Engineering** — Data Lakes, ETL/ELT, Streaming, Analytics, and Data Platforms.
- **Software Engineering** — Backend Systems, APIs, Distributed Architectures, and Cloud Infrastructure.
- **Artificial Intelligence** — Applied AI, Intelligent Automation, Machine Learning, and Data-driven Products.

Ultimately, I strive to build things that are not only technically sound but also useful, intentional, and built to last.
